For a previewing family (swe_fix) miners get the previews at open and the evaluated tasks at closeprivate repo
attestationsh-attestation-v2: sr25519(hotkey, "spark-hermes:<repo>:<round>:<bundle_sha256>:<signed_at>") in attestation.json. PR scopea strategy PR changes exactly one submissions/<hotkey>/ and nothing else (never sh/, config, another hotkey); its directory name must be a valid ss58. While a submission server is advertised (since r0005, 2026-09-23) it adds only attestation.json (and receipt.json): the prose is uploaded to the server, and a PR that adds prose is refused by CI and at the seal, with no fallback to the PR tree. The crown is merged only at the sealed head (--match-head-commit), so a push after the seal cannot slip unevaluated code into mainorchestrate.candidates, announce
S1 — copied answersa challenger sharing more than 8 distinct solution lines (≥ 24 chars) or more than 40 distinct 12-token runs with the round's private reference/alternate solutions and verifiers (for swe_fix: the code its bugs replaced) — text that also appears in a task's prompt, or in a preview's, excluded — is refused at seal.sh/validator/similarity.py
crownbest Δ credit vs baseline on this round's instances, > 0, ≥ 4 paired, not disqualified this round; among the eligible a tie does not dethrone the incumbent; other ties by pooled Δc then a round-keyed hash (not the hotkey name). A round where nobody beats the baseline crowns nobodysh/scoring/crown.py
dethronethe incumbent leaves submissions/ at announce only when (a) a challenger is crowned over it, (b) its pooled 8-round window fails the correctness gate on enough evidence (mean_d + z·se < 0, the evidence payment uses), or (c) it has gone 3 rounds in a row without the crown. A single round that crowns nobody does not dethrone (on 6 instances a better strategy ties the baseline by noise about a third of the time)orchestrate.dethroned
engine budgetone RTX 5090 serves two long agent contexts. A call the engine refuses for capacity is held by the proxy and retried (up to 240 s) instead of failing — a refusal would otherwise void the whole episode. The reference strategy (canon) runs only in calibration rounds, every 8th (--canon-every); it labels families and is never paid, and the pooled window carries its measurements between calibrationssh/validator/proxy.py, orchestrate.evaluate
rewardthe crowned pull request, merged at the sealed head, is what the reward follows (Gittensor workflow). An incumbent that wins is paid only through a defense PR (2026-09-24): an attestation-only PR re-signing the bundle it already defends with for this round (same digest); the seal keeps it as the incumbent and merges it if the crown holds. Without one, the incumbent keeps the crown and nothing is merged. The crown is decided on this round's instances alone: best mean Δ vs baseline > 0, ≥ 4 paired, not disqualified. score/Δc/weight are published diagnostics over the pooled window, not the reward, and read 0.000 at current sample sizessh/scoring/crown.py, orchestrate.announce
pooled statisticpooled over the last 8 rounds. score = the paired mean Δ of credit vs the baseline over the window, after the overfit and copy penalties (signed); weight = a score's share among the strategies above the baseline, summing to 1; Δc = the one-sided 90 % lower bound of that mean, published beside them as how sure the gain is. The efficiency term is off (w_e = 0): it penalised honest correctness gains and paid worse-than-baseline strategies for fewer calls. The reference variance is floored (max-entropy over n) so a degenerate all-identical null arm cannot make the baseline look certainsh/scoring/v2.py, --window-from
training dataSFT: the king's episodes where every check holds; DPO: the king's episode with credit ≥ 0.8 vs another surface's with at least 0.5 less, same instance; no king → nothing uploadedsh/exports/build.py

Indexed: 23,129 usable of 59,136 rows (the dataset as of 2026-09-17; docs/seed-datasets.md cites the paper's 50,137) (a problem statement, 1–10 broken tests, ≥ 5 passing, a diff that touches no test infrastructure). Rounds draw from 12 pure-Python repositories. Each bug is used at most onceprivate supply/swesmith.py, supply/families/swe_fix/repos.json
pilot20 bugs over 5 repositories, pinned model with no strategy, alone on the GPU: 17 valid, mean credit 0.34, 4 fully resolved, 4 partly, 9 not at all; no kept test broken; 9 of 17 used all 30 turns2026-09-17

Retired

  • terminal_task (family #3, FACET-Terminal-6k; retired before launch): real terminal tasks with pytest verifiers, transformed at mint, cheats = partial deliverables. Retired 2026-09-17: the pinned model earned ~0 credit on most tasks (r0003: 6 of 8 at 0 % for every surface) and screening at 10–70 % admitted none of its first screens. Its code stays in the private repo so rounds already minted can be evaluated.
  • posix_report (family #1, 2026-09-14): the pinned model solved it without a strategy; no headroom.
  • process_lifecycle (family #2; retired before launch): authored template; the baseline passed 7/8, one instance of headroom. Removed with its code on 2026-09-16 (family-process-lifecycle:pin = sha256:cb21736d…, hermes-base:pin = the python:3.12-slim agent image these ran on).
  • temperature 0.2 (era A0): repetition loops; see sampling above.
